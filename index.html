Data provenance & source imports

Running on embedded 2026 nominal GDP anchors and provisional demographic/macro inputs. Optional imports are processed locally in the browser (not uploaded).

Official sources

IMF: October 2026 WEO dataset, published 12–13 October 2026. 2026 baseline anchor: NGDPD (GDP, current US dollars). WEO country forecasts extend to 2031; the embedded file does not reproduce the full WEO 2027–2031 panel. Import the official workbook to enable it.
UN: World Population Prospects 2024, medium variant, population and total fertility rate. The 2024 revision is the available UN population projection source, and is not a 2026 revision.
Coverage: 50 largest economies based on included 2026 nominal GDP values, with Taiwan and Hong Kong treated as separate reporting economies.

Model equations & interpretation

Nominal USD GDP: GDP(t)=GDP(t−1) × (1+real growth) × (1+domestic GDP deflator)/(1+assumed local-currency depreciation versus USD). Population: use UN medium-variant July 1 population when imported, otherwise country-specific proxy exponential trends; fertility shock applies a smooth heuristic proportional change after 2026 (not a full cohort-component model). PPP per capita: starts from an illustrative 2026 PPP-to-nominal multiplier and converges slowly toward 1 with income growth; not official IMF PPP extrapolation. Scenarios: S1 −2pp, S2 −1pp, S3 0pp, S4 +1pp, S5 +2pp real annual growth adjustments, bounded below at −2%. Inflation/FX parameters are independent editable assumptions. All numbers in model years are scenarios, not probability-weighted forecasts.
Prepared as a transparent research model. October 2026 GDP values are sourced; all other preloaded economic and demographic inputs are analyst assumptions. Cross-country nominal GDP comparisons are particularly sensitive to cumulative exchange-rate assumptions. No investment or policy decisions should rely on these projections without validation.
